What this view is

  • Each source contributes a bounded slice — at most 50 entries — so this view can be shorter than what a source holds.
  • Counts cover only this view, after your search, filter, and anything you have hidden — never a whole source.
  • Entries are ordered by the time each source put on them, latest first within this view; an entry's time describes that entry only.
  • Mark seen and Dismiss hide entries from this Atrium view only. The source services are never changed, and hidden entries can reappear.
